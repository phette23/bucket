## How is [ insert X object ] a bucket?

Questions like this come from an impure place of _not looking at it right_. Give your contemplation some time to percolate and let the bucketness of the object fill your sensory apparatuses, a process known as "bucketing" after the "bracketing" operation of Husserlian phenomenological reduction. You may also benefit from reading our official [Abridged List of Things that are Buckets](./buckets.md). The absence of a thing from that list does not imply that it is not a bucket—an obvious absurdity—but merely that in the bucket of the universe there is a finite amount of spacetime in which to write the list of all buckets.
